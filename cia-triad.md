# The CIA Triad

## Introduction

The CIA Triad is a fundamental model in cybersecurity that describes three important principles used to protect information and information systems.

CIA stands for:

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

These principles help organizations protect sensitive information and maintain secure, reliable systems.

## 1. Confidentiality

Confidentiality means ensuring that information is accessible only to authorized individuals or systems.

### Example

A bank ensures that only an authorized customer can access their bank account information after successful authentication.

### Security Measures

- Strong passwords
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Encryption
- Access controls

### What Can Go Wrong?

If an attacker steals someone's password and accesses their private information, confidentiality has been compromised.

## 2. Integrity

Integrity means protecting information against unauthorized modification, destruction, or tampering.

Information should remain accurate, complete, and trustworthy.

### Example

If an attacker changes the amount of money in a financial transaction without authorization, the integrity of that information has been compromised.

### Security Measures

- File integrity monitoring
- Cryptographic hashes
- Access controls
- Digital signatures
- Audit logs

### What Can Go Wrong?

If an attacker modifies a company's records without permission, the integrity of those records is compromised.

## 3. Availability

Availability means ensuring that authorized users can access information and systems when needed.

### Example

A hospital must be able to access its computer systems and patient records when providing medical care.

### Security Measures

- Regular backups
- System maintenance
- Redundant systems
- Disaster recovery planning
- Protection against denial-of-service attacks

### What Can Go Wrong?

If a denial-of-service attack makes a website unavailable to legitimate users, its availability has been affected.

## Why the CIA Triad Matters

The CIA Triad helps security professionals identify and understand different types of security failures.

For example:

- Unauthorized disclosure of private information affects confidentiality.
- Unauthorized changes to information affect integrity.
- Disruption that prevents legitimate access affects availability.

A single cyberattack can affect more than one principle.

## Conclusion

Confidentiality, integrity, and availability are essential principles for protecting information and information systems.

Understanding these principles provides a foundation for studying cybersecurity threats, security controls, ethical hacking, and incident response.
